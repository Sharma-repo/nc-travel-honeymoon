January honeymoon guide

Why January is a brilliant month for your honeymoon

So, you have tied the knot, the confetti has settled and now it is time to focus on something that is completely about the two of you. A honeymoon in January is a brilliant choice because the world feels wide open. While everyone back home is tucked under blankets, you could be walking barefoot on a white beach, sipping wine on a vineyard terrace or watching snow fall outside your window as you sit by a roaring fire.

January honeymoons are about choice. You can chase sunshine, embrace snow, or mix the two. You might picture turquoise water and palm trees in the Maldives, fairy lights and Northern Lights in Lapland, or elephants at sunrise in Kenya. You might want a beach, a city, a mountain or a desert. Whatever your dream looks like, January has a place where it can come true.

My job is to make it effortless. I listen to what you both love and then design a honeymoon that fits. Every detail, from the flights to the private transfers, and those special moments you will never forget, is taken care of. You just turn up, relax and enjoy it all.

So, let’s explore what the world has waiting for you in January.

The Indian Ocean: Romance, colour and calm

The Maldives

If paradise had a postcode, this would be it. Think glassy turquoise lagoons, sand so soft it feels like silk and villas perched over the water where you can slip straight into the sea from your deck. In January, the Maldives is in its dry season, which means clear skies, calm seas and long days of sunshine. You can snorkel with rainbow-coloured fish, take a sunset cruise on a dhoni, or just drift through the week with no timetable at all.

It is a place where time slows down and every detail feels designed for two.

Weather: Around 30°C with 8 hours of sunshine and sea temperatures of 28°C.

Mauritius

Mauritius is that rare island that combines beach beauty with real depth. Behind its golden beaches are waterfalls, sugarcane fields and lush green hills. You can explore colourful markets in Port Louis, wander through botanical gardens or visit the Chamarel Waterfall. Food here is a joy, blending Creole, French and Indian flavours, and locals are famously warm and welcoming.

If you fancy a bit of adventure, go hiking in Black River Gorges or take a catamaran trip to Île aux Cerfs for a swim in crystal water.

Weather: Warm and tropical at 29°C, with gentle breezes and calm seas.

Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is made for couples who want to see and do a little of everything. You can explore ancient temples, tea plantations and wildlife-filled national parks before finishing on a beach. Picture yourself watching wild elephants at Minneriya, taking a scenic train through the hill country or learning to cook a curry with a local chef. The beaches on the south and west coasts, like Bentota and Galle, are golden and peaceful. January brings dry weather, bright skies and plenty of sunshine.

Weather: Around 30°C, with low humidity and ideal beach conditions.

The Caribbean: Sunshine with character

Grenada

Grenada is known as the Spice Island for good reason. You can smell nutmeg, cinnamon and cloves in the air as you explore the island. Grand Anse Beach stretches for miles, and the sea is so clear you can see tiny fish darting past your ankles. Away from the coast, the island is full of waterfalls, rainforest and hiking trails. The people are friendly, the rum is strong and the sunsets are some of the best in the Caribbean.

Weather: Around 28°C with dry days, calm seas and a gentle trade wind.

St Kitts and Nevis

These sister islands are small but full of charm. St Kitts has a lively local culture and lush rainforest to explore, while Nevis feels peaceful and private with its elegant plantation hotels. You can take the short ferry between them, spend your days exploring old sugar mills, or climb to viewpoints that look out over the Caribbean. It feels wonderfully untouched and romantic.

Weather: 27°C with low humidity and clear sunny skies.

Antigua

Antigua has a beach for every day of the year and most of them feel like they belong to you. The water is calm and turquoise, perfect for sailing or snorkelling. You can explore the island’s colonial history at Nelson’s Dockyard, take a jeep tour through the hills or relax at Shirley Heights with live music as the sun sets. There are luxurious all-inclusives and intimate boutique hotels, all with that laid-back Caribbean warmth.

Weather: 28°C with 9 hours of sunshine daily.

Anguilla

If you want peace and space, Anguilla is heaven. The beaches are powder soft, the food is exceptional, and everything feels effortless. You can take a boat to tiny Sandy Island for lunch, snorkel in clear water or spend your days being spoiled in a beautiful beach hotel. It has a quiet glamour that is ideal for honeymoons.

Weather: 26–29°C with light breezes and clear water for swimming.

Asia and Arabia: Adventure, colour and culture

Thailand

Thailand never disappoints. It has beaches, temples, rainforests, and that irresistible mix of relaxation and culture. January is one of the best months to visit, with dry warm weather across the south. You can explore the limestone cliffs of Krabi, island-hop in Phang Nga Bay or enjoy lazy evenings on Koh Lanta watching the sky turn pink. Add a few days in Chiang Mai or Bangkok for street food, temples and night markets. The food, the people and the atmosphere are simply unforgettable.

Weather: 27–30°C with calm seas and clear skies.

Oman

Oman is where Arabian romance meets adventure. Imagine a desert camp where you sleep under the stars, luxury resorts on the coast where the sea sparkles like sapphire, and ancient forts surrounded by palm-filled oases. You can explore Muscat’s colourful souks, drive through dramatic mountain scenery or swim in natural pools known as wadis. It feels exotic yet safe, authentic yet luxurious.

Weather: 25–28°C with dry, sunny days and cooler evenings.

Vietnam

Vietnam is a feast for the senses. You can sail through the limestone islands of Halong Bay, wander through lantern-lit streets in Hoi An, and eat pho at street-side stalls that smell of lemongrass and chilli. January is warm and dry in the south, making it a perfect time to explore. For beach time, head to Phu Quoc or Nha Trang.

Weather: 25–30°C with clear skies and low rainfall.

Africa: Wildlife, wine and wonder

South Africa

South Africa gives you everything in one trip. In Cape Town, you can hike up Table Mountain, wander through the V&A Waterfront and take a day trip to see penguins at Boulders Beach. Then drive to the Winelands for lazy lunches among the vineyards and a few days of wine tasting. The Garden Route is stunning at this time of year, with beaches, mountains and forests to explore. If you want a safari, head to the Eastern Cape for malaria-free reserves where you can see the Big Five.

Weather: Around 26°C with dry warm days and endless sunshine.

Kenya

Kenya in January is magical. The skies are clear, the plains are golden, and wildlife spotting is incredible. Stay in a romantic tented lodge, enjoy game drives at sunrise, and watch elephants or lions up close. You can then fly to the coast for a few relaxing days at Diani Beach, where the sand is white and the sea is warm.

Weather: 27–29°C with dry conditions and vivid sunsets.

Zanzibar

Zanzibar feels like stepping into another world. Spice farms, cobbled streets and turquoise lagoons make it both fascinating and relaxing. The beaches in the north around Nungwi and Kendwa are picture-perfect, and the water is ideal for snorkelling and diving. The food is fresh and full of flavour and the sunsets are unforgettable.

Weather: 30°C with sunshine and gentle breezes.

Europe: Snow, stars and winter magic

Lapland, Finland

Lapland is a fairytale in real life. The snow glistens, the air is crisp and the silence is magical. You can ride through the forest on a husky sled, visit a reindeer farm or warm up in a private sauna before watching the Northern Lights from your bed in a glass-roofed igloo. It is one of the most romantic winter experiences imaginable.

Weather: Between –10°C and –20°C, with clear nights and powder snow.

Tromsø, Norway

Tromsø is another wonderful place to see the Northern Lights but with a bit more buzz. The city has cosy cafés, great restaurants and plenty to do, from snowshoeing to whale watching. You can take a cable car to see the fjords at sunset and then chase the aurora in the evening.

Weather: Around –5°C with long dark nights and soft daylight.

Madeira, Portugal

If you prefer mild weather, Madeira is ideal. Known as the island of eternal spring, it is green, floral and peaceful. The coastal roads wind past little fishing villages and scenic viewpoints, and the local food is amazing. You can walk the levadas (irrigation channels) through the hills, explore Funchal’s markets or relax with a glass of Madeira wine as the sun sets.

Weather: 19–21°C with plenty of sunshine.

The Canary Islands

Tenerife, Lanzarote and Gran Canaria all enjoy warm, sunny weather in January. There are sandy beaches, volcanic landscapes and beautiful coastal walks. You can explore national parks, visit local wineries and enjoy evenings outdoors. It is perfect for couples who want warmth without long-haul travel.

Weather: 22–25°C with blue skies and light breezes.

Australasia: Down Under adventures

Australia

January is peak summer in Australia and it is buzzing with life. Sydney is full of sunshine, beach bars and that relaxed outdoor lifestyle. You can stroll along the harbour, climb the Sydney Bridge, or take a ferry to Manly for a laid-back afternoon by the sea.

Melbourne has a creative, foodie feel with lanes full of street art and hidden restaurants. Head to the Yarra Valley for vineyards and lazy picnics or drive the Great Ocean Road for stunning coastal views.

If you love nature, Queensland’s Great Barrier Reef is breathtaking, with vibrant coral and colourful marine life. The Whitsunday Islands are pure honeymoon bliss, with powder-soft sand and crystal water that feels almost unreal. Western Australia’s Margaret River region is perfect for wine lovers, while Perth offers that wonderful blend of beach and city energy.

Weather: 25–35°C depending on the region, long sunny days and warm evenings.

New Zealand

New Zealand is one of those places that takes your breath away, again and again. The scenery feels cinematic, with snow-capped peaks, emerald lakes and rolling vineyards, all in one day’s drive. January is summer here, so the weather is perfect for exploring.

On the South Island you can cruise through Milford Sound’s fjords, hike through national parks or take a helicopter over Franz Josef Glacier. Queenstown is brilliant for adventurous couples with everything from jet boating to bungee jumping, while the vineyards of Marlborough are perfect for long, lazy lunches.

On the North Island you can soak in natural hot springs in Rotorua, wander the art-filled streets of Napier or explore Auckland’s islands and beaches. Everywhere you go the people are welcoming and the landscapes feel untouched. It is a destination that makes you fall in love with the world all over again.

Weather: 22–28°C with long sunny days and clear, cool nights.

February honeymoon guide

February Honeymoons: Where romance, weather and timing align perfectly

February is one of my favourite months for honeymoons, and it’s one I recommend time and time again to my couples. You’re escaping the UK winter, travelling at a point where many destinations are at their absolute best, and often benefiting from better availability and value than later in the year.

What I love most about February honeymoons is that they’re rarely rushed or crowded.

Whether you want barefoot luxury, unforgettable wildlife moments, cultural depth or simply warm sunshine and time together, February offers some truly exceptional options when it’s planned properly. This is where experience matters.

Here’s my expert guide to the destinations that genuinely shine in February and why they work so beautifully for honeymoon travel.

Tropical perfection: Caribbean and Indian Ocean

February is peak season across the Caribbean and Indian Ocean, and it’s one of the most reliable times of year for weather. Warm days, low humidity, calm seas and sunshine you can actually plan around.

The Caribbean enjoys temperatures around 27–29°C, with low rainfall and bright, clear days.

Saint Lucia

Saint Lucia is a favourite for couples who want romance with wow factor. The Pitons create an unforgettable backdrop, while boutique resorts tucked into the rainforest offer privacy, spa experiences and those “just us” moments that honeymoons are all about. February is also excellent for snorkelling and sailing thanks to calm seas and great visibility.

Anguilla

Anguilla is one I often suggest to couples who want quiet luxury rather than nightlife. It’s refined, peaceful and all about space. Long white beaches, exceptional food and beautifully understated resorts make it ideal for couples who want to switch off properly.

The Maldives

The Indian Ocean really comes into its own in February. The Maldives is firmly in its dry season, with temperatures around 31–32°C and stunningly clear water. February is perfect for overwater villas, private pools and dining under the stars. It’s also one of the best months for couples who want absolute privacy and a true honeymoon feel.

Mauritius

Mauritius offers a wonderful balance of relaxation and exploration. You still get beautiful beaches, but with added culture, botanical gardens, waterfalls and rum distilleries. February is a great time for honeymoon upgrades here, something I always look out for when booking for my couples.

Wildlife, wine and wide open spaces: Africa

Africa is an incredible honeymoon choice in February, especially for couples who want experiences they’ll talk about for the rest of their lives.

East Africa is in its green season, which means fewer visitors, lush landscapes and incredible wildlife moments.

Kenya

Kenya is particularly special in February. This is when many animals give birth, so you’ll see young wildlife alongside dramatic predator action. The light is softer, the scenery greener and the experience feels far more intimate than peak migration months.

Tanzania

Tanzania offers the chance to witness the Great Migration in the Serengeti before finishing your honeymoon on the white sands of Zanzibar. I love this combination for couples who want adventure first, then total relaxation.

South Africa

South Africa works beautifully for February honeymoons as it's enjoying full summer. The Garden Route is vibrant and green, vineyards in Franschhoek and Stellenbosch are buzzing, and evenings are warm enough for long dinners and sunset wine tastings. It’s relaxed, romantic and surprisingly varied.

Culture, colour and calm seas: Asia

February is one of the most dependable months for travel across much of Asia, making it ideal for couples who want culture without challenging weather.

Thailand

Thailand sits comfortably in its dry season. February works just as well for lively resorts and island hopping as it does for slower paced spa focused honeymoons. It’s a destination I often tailor very carefully depending on the couple, as the experience can feel completely different depending on where you stay.

Vietnam

Vietnam is wonderfully atmospheric in February. Many couples love timing their honeymoon with Tet, the Lunar New Year, when streets are decorated, cities come alive and places like Hoi An glow with lantern light.

Borneo

Borneo is perfect for couples who want nature and something a little different. February offers drier conditions for rainforest lodges, wildlife encounters and truly memorable honeymoon experiences.

India

India is beautifully balanced in February. Rajasthan’s palace hotels, desert sunsets and historic cities feel indulgent rather than intense, making it a surprisingly romantic choice when planned properly.

Summer energy and big experiences: South America and Australia

February in the southern hemisphere is all about summer energy, long days and unforgettable scenery.

Brazil

Brazil is vibrant and full of life. Some couples choose to embrace the excitement of Rio Carnival, while others escape to quieter beach towns like Trancoso for a more relaxed but equally beautiful honeymoon.

Argentina

Argentina offers contrast in the best way. Buenos Aires is warm, stylish and romantic, while Patagonia’s glaciers and lakes are at their most accessible, making February ideal for couples who want variety.

Australia

Australia is hot, bright and full of possibilities. February suits reef experiences, coastal road trips and festival filled cities, especially for couples who want space, adventure and something a little epic for their honeymoon.

Shorter flights and seasonal romance

Not every February honeymoon needs to be long haul, and some of the most romantic experiences are closer than you think.

Dubai

Dubai offers guaranteed sunshine, warm seas and world class hotels without the long flight time. It works perfectly as a mini moon or luxury stopover.

Venice

Venice is magical in February thanks to Carnival. Masked balls, candlelit canals and a quieter city create a genuinely romantic atmosphere.

Iceland

Iceland is ideal for couples wanting something unforgettable. February offers excellent Northern Lights viewing, dramatic landscapes and geothermal hot tubs under star filled skies.

My February honeymoon advice

February honeymoons work best when they’re planned with care. Understanding weather patterns, knowing where to avoid crowds, securing the right room types and spotting honeymoon perks all make a huge difference to the experience.

I work closely with my couples from the very beginning. I take the time to understand what you actually want from your honeymoon, then match you to destinations that truly suit you, not just what looks good online. Every booking is fully ABTA and ATOL protected, and I’m with you every step of the way - before you travel, while you’re away and when you return.

If February is calling your name, let’s talk it through properly. I’ll help you choose the destination that fits your style, your pace and your idea of romance, so your honeymoon feels effortless from start to finish.

When you’re ready, I’m here.

March honeymoon guide

March honeymoons for couples who want something exceptional

Perfect timing. Beautiful weather. Experiences that feel considered, not copied.

March is a quietly brilliant month to honeymoon.

It’s not loud or obvious, but it knows exactly what it’s doing.

Across the world, destinations settle into their most comfortable rhythm. The heat hasn’t tipped into exhausting, storm seasons are nowhere near, and places feel calmer, more intimate and more authentic.

For couples who care about atmosphere, experience and weather, March is not a compromise month. It’s a confident choice.

This is where I guide couples who want their honeymoon to feel intentional, luxurious and genuinely memorable, whether that’s on land or at sea.

Indian Ocean romance

Warm seas, golden light and total escape

Maldives

March is one of the very best months in the Maldives. The dry season is in full swing, the skies are clear and the sea is at its calmest and most inviting.

Weather in March

Temperatures sit comfortably between 28 and 32°C.

Rainfall is low.

Seas are calm with excellent visibility for snorkelling and diving.

Most couples picture the Maldives as a villa based honeymoon, and that is magical. But for something truly unique, March is also the perfect time to experience the Maldives by sea.

Maldives by Cruise

Small ship and yacht style cruising here is intimate, rare and deeply romantic. Calm seas in March make sailing between atolls smooth and effortless.

You wake up anchored beside uninhabited islands. You snorkel straight from the deck. You picnic on sandbanks that disappear with the tide.

Where I’d steer you

A luxury dhoni or expedition style yacht with a small number of guests, followed by a few nights in an overwater villa to finish. This is the Maldives for couples who want something quietly extraordinary.

The Caribbean, perfectly timed

Sunshine, smooth sailing and island elegance

March is peak Caribbean season, and for honeymooners, that is exactly what you want. The weather is settled, humidity is low and the sea conditions are ideal.

Weather in March

Daytime temperatures hover around 27 to 30°C.

Rainfall is minimal.

Trade winds are gentle, keeping days comfortable.

Sea conditions are calm, making it one of the best months for cruising. This is when the Caribbean feels effortless.

Caribbean Honeymoon Cruises

March is one of the smartest months for a Caribbean honeymoon cruise. Smooth sailing conditions mean moving between islands feels relaxing rather than tiring, and ports feel vibrant without being overwhelming. Cruising allows you to experience multiple islands while keeping the comfort and rhythm honeymooners appreciate. You unpack once, slow the pace, and let each day unfold naturally.

Where I’d steer you

Routes that focus on islands with real romance and atmosphere.

St Lucia for dramatic scenery and sunset moments.

Antigua for calm bays and refined beach clubs.

Barbados for culture, food and understated sophistication.

Paired with a smaller, more refined ship, this becomes a honeymoon that feels polished, smooth and indulgent.

Africa with depth and drama

Honeymoons that stay with you

South Africa

March is one of South Africa’s most beautiful months. The intense summer heat fades, vineyards are in harvest and the overall pace feels relaxed and confident.

Weather in March

Warm days averaging 22 to 26°C.

Cooler evenings.

Dry, clear conditions in most regions.

This is a honeymoon built on contrast. Cape Town’s beaches and neighbourhood cafés. Wine tasting in the Winelands. Safari lodges where mornings start early and evenings end by firelight.

Where I’d steer you

A design led hotel in Cape Town, followed by a wine estate stay, then a private safari lodge. Balanced, elegant and unforgettable.

Tanzania and Zanzibar

March safaris are lush and cinematic. The landscape is green, wildlife viewing is excellent and the atmosphere feels raw and emotional. Zanzibar then softens everything. Warm seas, white sand beaches and slow, romantic evenings.

Where I’d steer you

A small, conservation focused safari camp, followed by a boutique beach retreat on Zanzibar’s quieter east coast.

Asia for couples who want meaning

Culture, beauty and intentional travel

Japan

March brings the start of cherry blossom season. It’s one of Japan’s most beautiful moments.

Weather in March

Cool, dry days around 10 to 15°C.

Comfortable for sightseeing and travel.

Japan is about contrast. Ultra modern cities paired with ancient rituals. Fast trains followed by moments of stillness.

Where I’d steer you

Tokyo for energy and design, Kyoto for tradition and romance, and at least one night in a traditional ryokan with private onsen bathing.

India

March is one of the last comfortable months to explore India before summer heat arrives.

Weather in March

Warm days without extreme heat.

Vibrant colours and seasonal festivals.

India is powerful, emotional and deeply memorable.

Where I’d steer you

The Golden Triangle paired with a luxury heritage stay, followed by calm in Kerala or Rajasthan’s countryside.

Closer to home, far from ordinary

Morocco

March is Morocco’s sweet spot. Warm days, cooler evenings and perfect conditions for exploring.

Where I’d steer you

A luxury riad in Marrakech, followed by the Atlas Mountains or Essaouira for balance and calm.

United Arab Emirates

Dubai in March feels polished and playful.

Weather in March

Daytime temperatures around 30°C.

Comfortable evenings for dining outdoors.

Where I’d steer you

Beachfront resorts offering space, privacy and elevated dining, or as a refined stopover paired with the Maldives.

Why March honeymoons feel different

March couples tend to travel with intention. They value weather. They value experience.

They value quality over crowds. And that is where the magic lives.

Let’s create something that feels like you. Whether that’s drifting between Maldivian atolls, island hopping in the Caribbean sunshine, watching wildlife at sunrise or immersing yourselves in culture somewhere unforgettable, I’ll help you shape a honeymoon that feels personal, beautiful and perfectly timed.

Because when it comes to honeymoons, the details matter.

And March really does get them right.

April honeymoon guide

Nikki's guide on where to honeymoon in April

April is one of my favourite months to plan honeymoons. It sits in that golden window where spring unfolds across the northern hemisphere, dry seasons linger in tropical paradises, and wildlife viewing peaks in some of the world’s most spectacular destinations.

As a UK-based honeymoon specialist, I’ve sent countless couples off in April and watched them come back with exactly the kind of trip they’d hoped for. Romantic, well-timed, and without the heavy crowds you get later in the year.

The key with April is timing. Easter can push prices up and availability down, so if you can travel just outside that window, you will usually find better value and a much more relaxed experience.

Let me walk you through the destinations that genuinely shine in April.

Africa

Morocco

April is Morocco’s sweet spot. Expect daytime temperatures around 23 to 26 degrees, with plenty of sunshine and comfortably cool evenings. You can wander through the souks of Marrakech, explore the blue streets of Chefchaouen or head into the Atlas Mountains where spring brings colour and freshness to the landscape.

Why it works for honeymooners:

Morocco is all about contrast. One day you are enjoying rooftop dining in a riad, the next you are drifting over desert landscapes or watching the sunset over the dunes. It feels different, immersive and incredibly romantic without being overly polished.

Adding Essaouira gives you a relaxed coastal balance, perfect for slowing things down together.

South Africa

April marks autumn in South Africa, with temperatures typically between 20 and 25 degrees and a dry, comfortable climate. The light turns golden, the pace slows, and it is one of the most enjoyable times to explore the country. You can combine Cape Town, the Winelands and safari all in one seamless trip. The Winelands are in harvest season, and Kruger offers excellent wildlife viewing.

Why it works for honeymooners:

Few destinations give you this level of variety in one trip. City, wine and safari all flowing naturally together. It feels like a journey rather than just a holiday, which makes it incredibly special for a honeymoon. Adding Victoria Falls gives you that real wow moment to finish.

Indian Ocean

Seychelles

April is one of the best months to visit the Seychelles, with temperatures around 29 to 30 degrees, low humidity and calm seas. It is ideal for snorkelling, relaxing, and enjoying the islands at their best.

Why it works for honeymooners:

This is barefoot luxury. Quiet beaches, crystal-clear water and complete privacy. It is all about switching off and just enjoying each other without distractions. Island hopping adds variety without losing that relaxed feel.

Maldives

April sits at the end of the dry season, with temperatures around 30 to 32 degrees and plenty of sunshine. Sea conditions are calm, and underwater visibility is excellent.

Why it works for honeymooners:

This is the classic honeymoon destination for a reason. Overwater villas, private dining and total seclusion. It is designed for romance and doing absolutely nothing, beautifully.

April also often gives slightly better value than peak months.

Asia

Japan

April brings mild temperatures of around 12 to 18 degrees and beautiful spring conditions. Cherry blossom season transforms the country, creating one of the most iconic travel experiences in the world.

Why it works for honeymooners:

It is romantic in a completely different way. Quiet moments, cultural experiences and those fleeting cherry blossom scenes that feel really special. It is not just a holiday, it is something you remember forever.

Bali

April marks the start of Bali’s dry season, with temperatures around 28 to 30 degrees and lower humidity. The island feels fresh, green, and ready to explore.

Why it works for honeymooners:

Bali is built for romance. Private villas, jungle views, beach clubs and spa experiences. It is easy to tailor and gives you that luxury feel without being complicated. Adding nearby islands can make it feel even more exclusive.

Thailand

April is hot, with temperatures often reaching 30 to 35 degrees, but the Gulf islands offer some of the best conditions. Calm seas and sunshine make it ideal for beach time.

Why it works for honeymooners:

Thailand gives you flexibility. You can combine culture, city life and beaches in one trip. It suits couples who want a bit of everything rather than just one experience.

Europe

Italy

April offers mild temperatures around 15 to 20 degrees, ideal for exploring and enjoying outdoor life. Cities feel alive again and the countryside starts to bloom.

Why it works for honeymooners:

It is classic romance without the crowds. You can take your time, enjoy long meals and really soak it all in. It feels relaxed and authentic, which is exactly what you want on a honeymoon.

Greece

April brings temperatures of around 18 to 22 degrees, with fewer tourists and a more authentic feel. The islands begin to open up and feel welcoming again.

Why it works for honeymooners:

You get Greece before the rush. Quiet streets, uninterrupted sunsets and a much more personal experience. It feels genuine rather than busy.

Caribbean and Central America

Caribbean Islands

April offers temperatures of around 28 to 30 degrees, with calm seas and plenty of sunshine. After Easter, the atmosphere becomes more relaxed with fewer crowds.

Why it works for honeymooners:

You get that perfect mix of relaxation and choice. Stunning beaches, great resorts and the option to explore or simply unwind. It is easy, reliable, and romantic.

Mexico

April is warm and dry, with temperatures around 28 to 32 degrees and ideal conditions for exploring. You can visit ruins, cenotes, and beaches all in one trip.

Why it works for honeymooners:

It offers more than just a beach. Culture, adventure and luxury all come together, making it perfect for couples who want variety.

Hawaii

April sees temperatures in the mid 20s, with the start of the dry season bringing great conditions across the islands. It is a brilliant time for exploring and island hopping.

Why it works for honeymooners:

Each island offers something different, so you can create a honeymoon that feels completely unique. It is perfect for couples who want variety and a bit of adventure.

Costa Rica

April is the final month of the dry season, with temperatures around 27 to 32 degrees and plenty of sunshine. Conditions are ideal for exploring both rainforest and coast.

Why it works for honeymooners:

It is for couples who want something a little different. Wildlife, adventure, and beautiful scenery all in one trip. It feels exciting as well as relaxing.

Why April works so well

April is one of those months where everything just comes together. You get better value in many destinations, fewer crowds than peak summer and excellent weather across multiple regions.

It is also a time where nature really shows off, from cherry blossoms to safari sightings and spring landscapes. It is calm, beautiful, and perfectly timed.

December honeymoon guide

Where to honeymoon in December: sunshine, snow and pure romance

There is something truly magical about getting married in December. It is a month filled with sparkle, celebration and new beginnings, which makes it the perfect time to start your married life somewhere wonderful.

Whether you dream of escaping the winter chill to bask in the sunshine or wrapping up together beneath a snowy sky, December offers some of the most romantic honeymoon destinations in the world.

As a honeymoon specialist, I love helping couples find that perfect balance between relaxation and adventure. Here are some beautiful ideas to help you imagine your December honeymoon and what makes each one so special.

Chasing The Sun

If you are craving sunshine, soft sand and warm sea breezes, December is one of the best months to travel. The weather is at its finest in many parts of the world, with the Caribbean, Indian Ocean and Asia all offering perfect beach days and calm seas.

The Maldives

Imagine waking up in the Maldives, opening your door to turquoise water and stepping straight from your villa into the lagoon. December marks the start of the dry season, bringing clear skies, low humidity and temperatures around 30 degrees. The sea is calm and inviting, and it is one of the best months of the year for diving and snorkelling. Couples can enjoy candlelit dinners on the sand or romantic boat trips at sunset. Every moment feels effortless and secluded.

Mauritius

Mauritius is another wonderful choice at this time of year. It is the beginning of summer on the island, with daily highs around 29 degrees and a warm, gentle sea. The landscape is green and full of colour, with waterfalls, forests and volcanic peaks waiting to be explored. You can visit the famous Chamarel Waterfall, wander through the Pamplemousses Botanical Gardens or simply relax on one of its perfect beaches with a cocktail in hand.

The island has a lovely balance of adventure and tranquillity, making it ideal for couples who want both.

The Seychelles

The Seychelles offers a similar sense of luxury but with a more natural, untouched charm. December brings long, sunny days with highs around 29 degrees and soft sea breezes. Island hopping is easy, so you can spend a few nights on Mahé before moving to Praslin or La Digue. Each island feels unique, from the palm-fringed beaches to the granite boulders and coral reefs. It is an idyllic escape where life slows down completely.

The Caribbean

If you prefer a livelier setting, the Caribbean is at its most beautiful in December. The hurricane season has ended, and the islands bask in eight hours of sunshine each day with average temperatures of 28 to 30 degrees. Saint Lucia is one of the most romantic spots in the Caribbean. The iconic Piton mountains rise dramatically above the coast, and the rainforest is alive with colour. You can explore hidden waterfalls, snorkel in clear blue water or enjoy dinner overlooking the sea.

Barbados is another wonderful choice, full of warmth, character and music. It is an island where you can sip rum punch at sunset, dine under the stars and feel completely relaxed.

UAE

If you prefer somewhere closer to home, Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer guaranteed sunshine in December. The weather is warm but comfortable, usually around 26 degrees during the day with cooler evenings. You can enjoy beach days, desert adventures or private dining on the waterfront. The city lights sparkle at night, and the views from the rooftop restaurants are breathtaking.

Many couples like to combine Dubai with the Maldives or Mauritius for a twin-centre honeymoon that mixes city excitement with island peace.

Adventure & Culture

For couples who love to explore, December is a fantastic month to travel across Asia and Australasia. The weather is dry and sunny, and the landscapes are full of life and colour.

Thailand

Thailand is especially beautiful at this time of year, with temperatures between 28 and 32 degrees. The skies are blue, the sea is calm and the beaches are in perfect condition. You can spend a few days in Bangkok exploring temples and floating markets, then fly south to unwind on the islands of Phuket, Koh Lanta or Krabi. The combination of culture, food and tropical scenery makes Thailand an endlessly romantic choice.

Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka also shines in December. The south and west coasts are warm and dry, with daytime temperatures between 27 and 30 degrees. It is a country full of history and heart. You can explore tea plantations in the hills, visit ancient temples or take a safari to see elephants and leopards. Afterwards, a few days by the beach is the perfect way to relax. Many couples love to pair Sri Lanka with the Maldives, as the short flight between the two makes for an unforgettable twin experience.

Bali

Further east, Bali remains a firm favourite for couples who want something spiritual and serene. Although December is technically part of the rainy season, the showers are usually brief and the temperature stays around 30 degrees. You can stay in a romantic villa surrounded by rice fields, try Balinese spa treatments and enjoy fresh seafood by candlelight. Bali offers wonderful value and an atmosphere that instantly puts you at ease.

New Zealand

If you are looking for dramatic landscapes and wide open spaces, New Zealand is at its most beautiful in December. It is the beginning of summer, with long days and temperatures between 20 and 28 degrees. The countryside is covered in wildflowers, and the lakes and mountains are simply breathtaking. It is perfect for couples who enjoy exploring, from road trips through the South Island to scenic boat cruises in Milford Sound.

Australia

Australia is also a dream destination in December, offering sunshine, festivals and vibrant energy. The weather varies by region, but you can expect daytime highs between 25 and 35 degrees. Sydney and Melbourne are alive with Christmas celebrations, and the beaches are at their best. You can snorkel on the Great Barrier Reef, taste wines in the Hunter Valley or watch the New Year fireworks over Sydney Harbour.

Safari & Sea

If your dream honeymoon involves wildlife, nature and unforgettable scenery, Africa is a spectacular choice.

South Africa

South Africa enjoys its summer season in December, with warm, dry days averaging 25 to 30 degrees. Cape Town is a city full of romance, framed by Table Mountain and the ocean. You can take the cable car for panoramic views, enjoy wine tasting in the vineyards of Stellenbosch and Franschhoek, and spend evenings dining by the sea. A safari in Kruger National Park is the perfect addition, with excellent wildlife viewing and luxurious lodges where you can unwind together after a day in the bush.

Kenya & Tanzania

Kenya and Tanzania are also wonderful at this time of year. The temperatures sit around 27 degrees with clear skies and plenty of sunshine. You can watch elephants and lions on the plains of the Maasai Mara or the Serengeti, enjoy sunrise from a hot air balloon and then fly to Zanzibar for a few days of pure relaxation. The sea stays around twenty eight degrees year-round, perfect for swimming, snorkelling and romantic beach walks.

Winter Wonderlands

For some couples, nothing feels more romantic than a snowy escape. December is the perfect month for crisp mornings, log fires and cosy evenings under the stars.

Lapland

Lapland in Finland is a magical choice for a winter honeymoon. The forests are blanketed in snow, the lakes are frozen and the air is beautifully crisp. Temperatures often hover around minus five degrees, creating a true fairytale atmosphere. You can stay in a glass igloo, ride through the snow on a husky sled and even meet Santa in his hometown of Rovaniemi. The Northern Lights often dance across the sky, adding to the wonder.

Iceland

Iceland offers a similar sense of magic with temperatures between zero and five degrees. It is a land of waterfalls, glaciers and hot springs. You can bathe in the Blue Lagoon while the snow falls around you or head out on an evening tour to see the Northern Lights. It feels otherworldly yet peaceful, making it a honeymoon like no other.

Switzerland

Switzerland is another beautiful choice in December. The mountains are covered in snow and the villages glow with festive lights. Temperatures are usually between two and six degrees in the valleys and colder in the high resorts. It is perfect for skiing, spa retreats or simply strolling through quaint towns with a cup of hot chocolate.

Canada

Canada also shines in December. Vancouver has a wonderful blend of city and mountain life, while Whistler offers world-class skiing. The temperatures range from minus five to three degrees, so it is cold enough for snow but not too harsh for exploring. You can enjoy sleigh rides, cosy lodges and picture-perfect scenery.

UK

Closer to home, London sparkles at this time of year. The air is crisp, usually around five to eight degrees, and the city feels alive with festive cheer. You can wander through Christmas markets, go ice skating at Somerset House and take in the views from the London Eye at sunset. It is a beautiful setting for a short honeymoon or a romantic mini break.
