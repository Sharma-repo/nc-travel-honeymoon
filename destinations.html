European honeymoons with NC Travel

Not every honeymoon has to be long haul. Europe has some truly magical spots for newlyweds. Full of charm, history, amazing food and unforgettable views. From the romantic streets of Paris to the sunsets of Santorini, you’re never short of incredible places to explore as a married couple.

If you’re looking for something shorter, more budget friendly or packed with things to do, a European honeymoon could be just right for you. Whether it’s luxury or laid-back, beach or city, I’ll help you plan it all with none of the stress.

No fees just personal advice, honest guidance and a honeymoon built around you.

Why honeymoon in Europe

Europe is ideal if you want a honeymoon that is easy to reach but full of variety. It offers

Whether you want to stroll through cobbled streets, sip wine in the sun or swim in crystal-clear seas, it is all here in Europe.

  • Romantic cities and stunning coastlines
  • Incredible food and wine experiences
  • Charming boutique hotels and stylish resorts
  • Scenic train journeys, cultural sights and picture-perfect views
  • The option to twin two destinations in one trip
  • Less flight time so more time to enjoy yourselves
Best time to go

You can honeymoon in Europe any time of year. Here is a quick guide

I will help you pick the best destination and time based on what you want to do.

  • Spring from April to June brings warm weather, blooming flowers and fewer crowds
  • Summer in July and August is hot and lively, great for beaches and alfresco dining
  • Autumn from September to October is perfect for wine regions and city breaks, still warm but more relaxed
  • Winter from November to March is ideal for cosy hideaways, Christmas markets and skiing
Top European honeymoon destinations

Santorini

Whitewashed villages, blue-domed churches and stunning sunsets. Ideal for couples who want a wow factor with romance and views

Amalfi Coast

Italy at its best. Colourful cliffside towns, amazing food, boat trips and boutique hotels overlooking the sea

Paris

Classic romance. Think candlelit dinners, art galleries, strolls by the Seine and a little luxury in the city of love

Barcelona

A vibrant mix of beach, architecture, tapas and nightlife. Perfect if you want a bit of everything

Lake Como

Elegant and peaceful. Lakeside villas, mountain views and Italian charm all rolled into one

Croatia

Stunning coastal towns, island hopping, waterfalls and old-world charm. A great mix of adventure and relaxation

Portugal

Beautiful beaches in the Algarve, the culture and wine of Porto or the romantic backstreets of Lisbon

Iceland

For something totally different. Chase the Northern Lights, relax in the Blue Lagoon and explore dramatic landscapes

Things to do on a European honeymoon
  • Watch the sunset from a rooftop bar in Rome
  • Sail between Croatian islands or along the Amalfi Coast
  • Take a scenic train ride through the Swiss Alps
  • Explore castles and vineyards in the French countryside
  • Wander cobbled streets hand in hand in a medieval old town
  • Soak in a thermal spa or hot spring after a day of exploring
  • Try wine tasting in Tuscany or cava tasting in Catalonia
  • Or just unwind at a peaceful coastal retreat
Accommodation styles

Whether you are looking for five-star hotels or hidden boutique stays, Europe has it all

I’ll help you find something that feels just right for the two of you.

  • Romantic city hotels in old town settings
  • Coastal resorts with private balconies and sea views
  • Family-run boutique stays full of local charm
  • Luxury spa hotels in wine regions or mountains
  • Quirky and unique places like cave hotels or converted castles
What you should know
  • Europe is easy to reach with short flight times from the UK
  • No visas needed for most destinations if you have a UK passport
  • Currency varies so I will guide you on what to prepare
  • Many places are perfect for self-drive or rail holidays if you want to explore
  • You can twin two or more places to mix beach and city or culture and coast

Asia honeymoons with NC Travel

If you are dreaming of golden temples, tropical beaches and unforgettable experiences, an Asia honeymoon could be exactly what you are looking for. With exotic scenery, vibrant culture and incredible food, this part of the world offers the perfect blend of romance, adventure and luxury.

From the dreamy shores of Bali and the opulence of Dubai to the peaceful beauty of Thailand and the rich culture of Vietnam, Asia is full of magical places made for couples. Whether you want a relaxing beach break, a cultural adventure or something in between, I will help you create a honeymoon that suits you perfectly.

Planning your Asia honeymoon does not need to be stressful. I will make it personal, simple and completely tailored to you. No extra fees, no call centres and no time wasted searching online. Just honest advice, expert knowledge and a trip built around you.

Why honeymoon in Asia
  • Warm weather, beautiful beaches and lush landscapes
  • Rich culture and history with temples, palaces and local traditions
  • A fantastic mix of relaxation and adventure
  • Romantic resorts, private villas and luxury hideaways
  • Unique food experiences from street markets to fine dining
  • Great value for money in many destinations

Whether you are sipping cocktails at sunset or exploring colourful temples together, Asia offers everything you need for an unforgettable honeymoon.

When is the best time to go

Asia is a year-round destination, but the weather varies depending on where you go. Here is a quick guide:

Bali

Best from April to October for dry, sunny weather November to March is the wet season but still warm and tropical

Thailand

West coast areas like Phuket and Krabi are best from November to April East coast destinations like Koh Samui are best from May to September

Dubai

Comfortable from October to April May to September is very hot but quieter and more affordable

Vietnam

North Vietnam is best in spring and autumn Central Vietnam is ideal between February and August South Vietnam is warm and sunny all year round

I will always recommend the best places based on when you want to travel.

Top Asia honeymoon destinations

Bali

Tropical, spiritual and incredibly romantic. Think rice terraces, jungle swings, beach clubs and private villas. Perfect for couples who want a mix of relaxation, culture and wow-factor scenery

Thailand

Warm, welcoming and full of variety. Enjoy stunning beaches, boutique resorts, island-hopping and cultural sites. Ideal for a classic beach honeymoon with a twist

Dubai

Luxury, style and sunshine. Expect glamorous hotels, rooftop dining, private desert dinners and indulgent spa time. Great for a short break or as part of a twin-centre honeymoon

Vietnam

For couples who love to explore. Cruise through Halong Bay, wander through historic towns, relax on the beach and enjoy incredible local food. A brilliant option for a cultural and scenic adventure

  • Explore colourful night markets and try amazing street food
  • Relax in a private villa with your own plunge pool
  • Take a couples’ spa day with traditional treatments

Indian Ocean honeymoons with NC Travel

If you’re picturing barefoot luxury, crystal-clear lagoons and total privacy, an Indian Ocean honeymoon could be just the ticket. With postcard-perfect beaches, warm turquoise waters and stunning overwater villas, this part of the world is made for romance.

From the dreamy Maldives and the lush beauty of Mauritius to the wild charm of Sri Lanka and the untouched beaches of the Seychelles, every island in the Indian Ocean has its own unique magic.

This is where once-in-a-lifetime honeymoons come to life. And I’m here to make it all simple, personal and stress free. No added fees, no endless searching and no call centres. Just trusted, expert advice and a honeymoon built around you.

Why honeymoon in the Indian Ocean
  • Powder-soft beaches and clear warm seas that feel like stepping into a postcard
  • Luxury accommodation ranging from beachfront villas to overwater bungalows
  • World-class snorkelling and diving with coral reefs and colourful marine life

  • Candlelit dinners on the beach and spa treatments with ocean views
  • Warm, friendly hospitality and exceptional service

It is all about slow mornings, lazy afternoons and those just married moments that you will remember forever.

When is the best time to go

The Indian Ocean has warm weather year-round, but each island has its own seasons. Here is a quick guide:

Maldives

Best between November and April for dry, sunny days and calm seas Expect short tropical showers from May to October, especially in July and August

Mauritius

Warm and sunny from May to December January to March can be rainy but is still a good time for beach lovers who do not mind the occasional shower

Seychelles

A year-round destination with good weather most of the time April, May, October and November are the most settled and popular months

Sri Lanka

December to March is best for the west and south coasts April to September is better for the east coast

I will always recommend the right island based on the time of year you want to travel.

Top Indian Ocean honeymoon destinations

Maldives

The ultimate in barefoot luxury. Think private villas, overwater bungalows, incredible snorkelling and service that makes you feel like royalty. Ideal for couples who want to relax in style

Mauritius

Tropical, colourful and full of charm. Great beaches, lush scenery, welcoming people and a great mix of adventure and relaxation

Seychelles

Perfect for island-hopping and postcard scenery. Granitic rocks, white sand beaches, nature reserves and a slower pace make this a dream for couples who want beauty and space

Sri Lanka

For couples who want to mix beach with culture. You can explore ancient temples, take a safari, visit tea plantations and still enjoy beautiful beaches. Great for twin-centre honeymoons

  • Or simply relax, recharge and celebrate being newly married

Caribbean honeymoons with NC Travel

If you’re dreaming of turquoise waters, golden beaches, swaying palms and a cocktail in hand, then a Caribbean honeymoon could be just what you're looking for. The Caribbean has that laid-back charm, stunning scenery and a sense of escape that makes it one of the most popular honeymoon spots for good reason.

With island after island offering something a little different, it’s easy to find somewhere that matches your vibe as a couple. Whether you want total relaxation, a touch of adventure or a mix of both.

And the best bit? I’ll help you find the right island, the perfect hotel and all the little extras to make it your own. Completely personal, completely stress free and never any added fees.

Why choose the Caribbean for your honeymoon

The Caribbean is all about slowing down, switching off and enjoying your time together as newlyweds. It offers:

It’s the kind of place where time slows down and you can really focus on each other.

  • Unspoilt beaches with soft white sand and warm, crystal-clear waters
  • Incredible snorkelling, diving and marine life for the water lovers
  • Romantic sunset cruises and private dining on the beach
  • Beautiful boutique hotels and luxury all-inclusives with five-star service
  • Island culture full of colour, music, local food and a warm welcome
  • An easy-going vibe that makes it feel like you’ve truly escaped the world
When is the best time to visit the Caribbean

The Caribbean is warm all year round which is great for honeymoons, but there are a few things to keep in mind.

  • December to April is the dry season and generally the best time to go. Think sunny skies and perfect beach weather
  • May to June can be a little more humid but still great value with fewer crowds
  • July to November is hurricane season so it is best to travel with good insurance and choose your island carefully. I will always guide you on what’s best and safest
Top Caribbean honeymoon spots

Here are just a few of the Caribbean islands my couples love:

St Lucia

Lush, green and romantic with its iconic Piton mountains, natural hot springs and even a drive-in volcano. Great for couples who want a mix of beach and adventure

Barbados

Classy, fun and full of culture. You’ve got luxury beach resorts, buzzing nightlife in St Lawrence Gap and local rum distilleries to explore

Antigua

Famous for having 365 beaches, one for every day of the year. It’s perfect for yacht trips, snorkelling and soaking up the sun in peaceful coves

Grenada

A quieter gem with a more authentic feel. Known as the Spice Island, it’s home to waterfalls, rainforest hikes and the incredible Underwater Sculpture Park

Cuba

Add a little spice and culture to your honeymoon. Wander the streets of Havana, soak up the history and then relax on the beautiful beaches of Varadero

Jamaica

Laid-back and full of energy. Think reggae, waterfalls, jerk chicken and luxury cliffside hotels with sunset views you’ll never forget

St Kitts and Nevis

A beautiful twin-island escape with a relaxed, uncrowded feel. Perfect for couples who want to slow the pace. Expect lush rainforest, historic charm, volcanic views and some truly breathtaking beaches

What you can do on a Caribbean honeymoon
  • Charter a private boat and snorkel in hidden lagoons
  • Indulge in spa treatments by the sea
  • Explore local markets, waterfalls or colourful towns
  • Enjoy candlelit dinners on the beach
  • Island hop or go for a twin-centre break with two totally different vibes
  • Go zip-lining, hiking or scuba diving if you fancy some adventure
  • Or simply switch off, relax and enjoy each other’s company
Styles of accommodation

Whether you’re after a small boutique hotel tucked into the hillside or a sprawling all-inclusive with swim-up suites, I can find something to suit your style and budget

  • Adults-only all-inclusives with everything taken care of
  • Chic eco-resorts for a more natural, off-the-beaten-track feel
  • Private villas and luxury beach bungalows
  • Big-name resorts with all the bells and whistles
  • Quiet, romantic hideaways with real local charm
Good to know before you go
  • Most Caribbean islands require a valid passport and some may need visas depending on where you are flying from
  • Currency and plug sockets vary island to island but I will guide you through the essentials
  • Flights are around eight to ten hours from the UK depending on your destination
  • Travel insurance is a must especially if you are travelling during the wetter months
  • You can combine two islands in one trip if you fancy a bit of variety

Not all honeymoons are about doing nothing

Yes, honeymoons are meant to be relaxing, but that doesn’t mean they all need to be spent by the pool with a book and a cocktail. Some couples unwind in their own way. That might mean exploring somewhere new, heading off the beaten track or ticking something unforgettable off their bucket list.

If you are the kind of couple who would rather pack hiking boots than flip flops, I can help.

At NC Travel, I create honeymoons that match your style. Whether that means total chill, full-on adventure or something in between, I’ve got you covered.

Ideas for honeymoons with a twist

Canada

Snowy mountains, frozen lakes and romantic log cabins. Ski at Lake Louise, explore Banff National Park and cosy up with hot chocolate after a day on the slopes. A perfect choice for winter-loving couples and outdoor fans.

Antarctica

One for the bucket list. Expedition cruises, icebergs, penguins and complete remoteness. It is cold, bold and completely unforgettable. If you want a honeymoon that no one else has done, this is it.

South Africa

See the Big Five on safari, relax in a luxury lodge, enjoy the food and wine of Cape Town and finish with beach time in Mauritius or Mozambique. An adventurous twin-centre with a touch of glam.

Costa Rica

Zip-line through the jungle, spot sloths in the wild, relax in hot springs and stay in eco lodges in the rainforest. A brilliant balance of nature, adventure and romance.

Norway

Husky sledding, Northern Lights, glass-roofed igloos and fjord views. Magical in winter and peaceful in summer. Perfect for couples who want something out of the ordinary.

New Zealand

Incredible road trips, lakes, mountains, vineyards and adventure sports. You can hike, kayak, see glow worms, spot whales and still enjoy a soak in a hot tub under the stars.

Patagonia

Dramatic and wild. Towering peaks, glaciers, hiking trails and remote lodges where it is just the two of you and the landscape. A real off-grid adventure.
