Your honeymoon journey with NC Travel

From the first chat to the welcome-home message.

Booking your honeymoon should feel as exciting as planning the wedding day. Nikki’s six-stage process keeps it relaxed, flexible and personal.

Let’s chat

Phone, Zoom, coffee or even a glass of champagne — the first step is getting to know you both and hearing your ideas. Nikki can come to you, you can visit her, or you can meet somewhere that suits you both. This is where the bond of trust begins.

  • Accommodation style
  • Travel dates and weather
  • Budget and priorities
  • Dream destinations
  • Experiences and extras
01

Your tailored ideas

Nikki turns the conversation into honest destination and hotel suggestions. You can tweak, adjust or completely change the plan as many times as needed — you are never on the clock. This is your honeymoon, possibly the most important holiday you will ever take together, so it has to feel just right.

02

Making it official

Once you love the plan, Nikki handles the bookings and admin.

  • Flights, hotels and transfers
  • Upgrades, excursions and room requests
  • Honeymoon fund and gift-list setup
  • Hotel notification and thoughtful room touches where possible
03

Before you travel

Documents can be posted or shared electronically, and Nikki can meet you to go through everything together.

  • Printed document pack
  • Travel wallet and luggage tags
  • Hand-luggage liquids bag
  • A small present
  • And, importantly, sweets for the plane

Restaurant ideas, excursions, spa suggestions or reassurance before departure are all part of the support.

04

While you’re away

Nikki sends a message on the day you travel and remains contactable by mobile, WhatsApp, email, home number and social media, with an out-of-hours number for peace of mind.

05

When you’re home

You’ll receive a welcome-home message. Nikki loves hearing what you enjoyed and seeing favourite photos — every honeymoon story adds to the personal service she gives future couples.

Frequently asked questions

Questions couples usually ask before we start.

No pressure and no confusing small print — just the practical answers you need before deciding what comes next.

Does using you cost more?

No. My service is free to you - I'm paid commission by the operators, and because I compare 400+ of them I often beat the price you've found yourself.

We've already found a deal online - can you still help?

Send it over. I'll check what's really included and either match it properly protected, or tell you honestly if it's already a good one.
